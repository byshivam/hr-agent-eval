# Tayal Capital — Leave Policy

*Tayal Capital is a fictional company. This document exists only to test an HR AI agent.*

## [LV-01] Leave types and annual entitlement

Every full-time employee receives the following leave each calendar year:

- **Casual leave (CL):** 8 days
- **Sick leave (SL):** 10 days
- **Earned leave (EL):** 18 days

Unused casual and sick leave lapse on 31 December. Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward to the next year.

## [LV-02] Casual leave rules

Casual leave is for short personal needs. A single casual leave request can cover at most **3 working days**. Casual leave must be applied for **before** the leave starts — it cannot be applied on the same day or after the fact.

## [LV-03] Sick leave rules

Sick leave needs no advance notice. It may be applied for up to **7 days after** the absence. A sick leave request of **more than 2 working days** must be supported by a medical certificate, uploaded within 3 days of returning to work.

## [LV-04] Earned leave rules

Earned leave is for planned time off such as holidays and family events. It must be applied for at least **7 calendar days before** the leave starts. Requests of more than 10 working days need approval from the department head in addition to the reporting manager.

## [LV-05] How leave days are counted

Only working days are deducted from a leave balance. Saturdays, Sundays and company holidays falling inside a leave period are **not** counted. A request whose dates contain no working day is rejected.

## [LV-06] Approval and cancellation

All leave requests go to the employee's reporting manager for approval. A request stays **pending** until the manager approves or rejects it — a submitted request is not an approved request. Employees can cancel a pending or approved request before its start date; the days are returned to the balance.

## [LV-07] Parental leave

- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks of paid leave.
- **Paternity leave:** 5 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- **Adoption leave:** 12 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver.

## [LV-08] Leave without pay

When all relevant leave balances are used up, an employee may request leave without pay (LWP). LWP is granted at HR's discretion and must be requested by raising an HR ticket.
