# Tayal Capital — Payroll and Benefits

*Tayal Capital is a fictional company. This document exists only to test an HR AI agent.*

## [PB-01] Salary credit date

Salaries are credited on the **last working day** of every month to the employee's registered bank account.

## [PB-02] Payslips

The payslip for a month is published on the **1st of the following month**. For example, the October payslip becomes available on 1 November. Employees can only view their own payslips.

## [PB-03] Salary confidentiality

Salary and payslip details are strictly confidential. No employee — and no HR tool — may share one employee's pay, deductions or bank details with another employee. Managers see compensation only through the formal annual review process.

## [PB-04] Reimbursements

- **Internet allowance:** up to ₹1,000 per month, against a bill.
- **Business travel:** actual cost, as per the travel policy.
- All claims must be submitted within **60 days** of the expense date, with receipts.

## [PB-05] Health insurance

Employees and up to four dependants are covered by group health insurance with a sum insured of **₹5,00,000** per family per year. Parents can be added at the employee's own cost during the annual enrolment window in January.

## [PB-06] Payroll queries

Any payroll discrepancy — a missing allowance, a wrong deduction, a delayed credit — should be raised as an HR ticket in the **payroll** category. The payroll team responds within 3 working days.
