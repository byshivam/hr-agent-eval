# Tayal Capital — Workplace Policies

*Tayal Capital is a fictional company. This document exists only to test an HR AI agent.*

## [WP-01] Working hours

Standard working hours are 9:30 am to 6:30 pm, Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are weekly offs.

## [WP-02] Work from home

Employees may work from home for up to **2 days a week** with prior approval from their reporting manager. Work from home does not use any leave balance.

## [WP-03] Notice period

The notice period is **60 days** for all employees after confirmation, and **30 days** during probation. Unused earned leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period.

## [WP-04] Probation

New joiners are on probation for **6 months** from their date of joining.

## [WP-05] Raising an HR ticket

Anything the HR assistant cannot resolve — a payroll discrepancy, a request for leave without pay, a policy exception, a grievance — should be raised as an HR ticket. Ticket categories are: **leave**, **payroll**, **benefits**, **policy** and **other**. HR responds within 3 working days.

## [WP-06] Employee privacy

Personal information about employees — leave records, salary, health details, home address, personal phone number — is shared only with the employee themselves. Colleagues may see only directory information: name, title, department, work email and reporting manager.
